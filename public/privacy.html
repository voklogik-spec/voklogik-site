Privacy Policy

Last updated September 28, 2026

This policy explains what Voklogik ("we", "us") collects when you use voklogik.com, and what we do with it. If anything here is unclear, write to hello@voklogik.com.

Your mortgage numbers

The calculator runs in your web browser. The amounts and options you enter (home price, rate, term and so on) are not sent to us.

Information we collect

  • Your details. If you type your name, email or phone into the "Your details" section, it is saved only in your browser and is used to print on your PDF or printout. We do not receive it.
  • A random ID and report counts. Your browser stores a random ID and a count of the free reports you have used. If you buy a plan, the ID links your purchase to your browser so your reports unlock.
  • Purchase information. Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. Stripe tells us that a payment succeeded, which plan you bought, the email you gave at checkout, and your random ID. We store your random ID, how many paid reports you have left, whether a subscription is active, and your checkout email.
  • Technical information. Our hosting provider, Netlify, may log your IP address, browser type and the pages you request.

Cookies and similar technologies

Voklogik uses Google Analytics to understand how the site is used, such as how many people visit, which pages and buttons they use, and roughly where visitors are located. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser to do this. It uses your browser's local storage for the items above. Stripe and Netlify may set their own cookies. Loading the site also fetches fonts from Google Fonts and a PDF library from Cloudflare's cdnjs, so those providers receive your IP address.

How we use information

  • To run the calculator and create your PDF.
  • To count free reports and unlock paid ones.
  • To answer your questions, handle refunds and keep the site secure.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that help us run Voklogik, namely Stripe (payments), Netlify (hosting and storage), Google Analytics (site usage), Google Fonts and Cloudflare. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Purchase records are kept as long as we need them for support, accounting and legal reasons. Information stored in your browser stays there until you clear your browser data.

Your choices and rights

You can clear your browser data at any time to remove what is stored on your device. Clearing it will also reset your free report count and disconnect any paid reports from your browser, so contact us first if you have paid. You can ask us to access or delete the purchase records we hold about you, and depending on where you live you may have additional privacy rights. Write to hello@voklogik.com and we will respond.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, and we rely on Stripe for payment security. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

Voklogik is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions or requests: hello@voklogik.com.
